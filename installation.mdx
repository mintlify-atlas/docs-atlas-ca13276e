Stratiles is an iOS app that runs on iPhone and iPad. Follow the steps below to install the app on your device.

## Requirements

- iOS 17.0 or later
- An active Strava account with activity data
- Internet connection for initial setup and data syncing

## Installation methods

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download from TestFlight">
    Stratiles is currently available through Apple's TestFlight beta testing program.
    
    1. Install the TestFlight app from the App Store if you don't already have it
    2. Get the TestFlight invitation link for Stratiles
    3. Open the link on your iOS device
    4. Tap "Accept" to join the beta
    5. Tap "Install" to download Stratiles
    
    <Note>
    TestFlight builds expire after 90 days. You'll receive notifications when new builds are available.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Wait for App Store release">
    Stratiles will be available on the App Store soon. Once released, you'll be able to download it like any other iOS app:
    
    1. Open the App Store app
    2. Search for "Stratiles"
    3. Tap "Get" to download and install
    
    <Info>
    App Store availability coming soon. Check back for updates!
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## First launch

When you first open Stratiles, you'll see a welcome screen with a preview heatmap and the app name. The interface is designed with a dark theme and uses Strava's signature orange color for accents.

<Note>
Stratiles requires authentication with your Strava account to function. You'll be prompted to connect your account on first launch.
</Note>

## Verify installation

After installing Stratiles, you should see:

- The Stratiles app icon on your Home Screen
- A dark-themed interface when you open the app
- A "Connect with Strava" button on the login screen
- A privacy notice stating "We only read your activity data. Nothing is stored on our servers."

## Troubleshooting

### App won't install

- Ensure you're running iOS 17.0 or later
- Check that you have enough storage space on your device
- Try restarting your device and attempting installation again

### TestFlight link doesn't work

- Make sure you have the TestFlight app installed
- Check that the invitation link hasn't expired
- Contact the developer for a fresh invitation link

### App crashes on launch

- Force quit the app and relaunch it
- Restart your device
- Reinstall the app from TestFlight
- Report the issue to the developer with details about your device model and iOS version
